The Story Behind India's Softest Cookies !

Hi, we're Arushi and Chaman—founders, cookie lovers, and the heart behind Dohful. Our love story with cookies started on our honeymoon across Europe, where soft-baked cookies became a daily ritual. Returning to India, we couldn’t find anything that came close. So, we rolled up our sleeves and got to baking. After 70 days and 300+ trials in our tiny home kitchen, the perfect cookie was born. Not just soft and gooey— but bold, nostalgic, and unforgettably Dohful.

Why Dohful?

Coming up with the name “Dohful” wasn’t exactly a piece of cake—or cookie. It took us over three months, countless name ideas, and one very unexpected discovery to land on it. Our first favorite? “Dookie.” Cute, catchy, and close to “cookie,” we thought it was perfect—until we found out it also means... poop. Needless to say, we had to start from scratch (and scrap the logo we were already designing).

After weeks of circling names like Doughey, Gooiness, and SleepyDo (none quite right), “Dohful” finally clicked. A play on “dough” and “fulfilling,” it felt just right—wholesome, warm, and happy, just like our cookies. Since then, we’ve heard it countless times: “That’s such a sweet name.” Every time, it makes our hearts (and spatulas) do a little happy dance.
